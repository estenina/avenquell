Ad Diagnostic

$3,500

Your ad spend is running. Results aren't following. This diagnostic traces the path from ad to lead — and finds where it breaks.

We work from your exports, not your logins. You send campaign exports, analytics pulls, and screenshots of your settings — taken in the last seven days, not from memory. From those, we read what's actually configured: whether conversions are set to a button click instead of a real submission, whether calls are counted at all, whether your sources are tagged, whether your spend is chasing duplicated events. We name the problem, rank what to fix first, and include 3–5 ad concepts built around the diagnosis — so the report ends with something you can act on, not just read. Signed by Iryna.

What you upload

Required:

  • Campaign exports from Meta Ads Manager or Google Ads — CSV or XLSX, 60–90 days (not report screenshots)
  • GA4 export for the same period
  • Average order value and rough margin
  • What your business counts as a conversion
  • Screenshots of your settings screens taken in the last 7 days: goals and conversions, bid strategy, audiences

Helpful:

  • CRM deal export with sources, 90 days
  • Call tracking export, 60–90 days

What we can't verify from your uploads

Screenshots show configuration. They don't show behavior. Without live access, we can't confirm that a conversion event actually fires on a real lead submission — only that it's set to something. We also can't verify real audience sizes inside your ad account, whether your Meta Conversions API is sending quality signals, whether your pixel fires correctly on live traffic, how your tracking behaves across domain transitions, or the history of settings changes over time. These gaps are noted in the report.

Guarantee

We name the cause — or we refund you. Applies when the full required upload was complete and the data did not support a named diagnosis. Items listed above under “What we can't verify” are excluded from refund scope — they are disclosed before you pay.

Credit

The diagnostic fee is credited against your next project if you move forward within two weeks of receiving the report: fully toward a Conversion Foundation or Campaign Build, half toward the first month of Paid Media Management — up to the cost of that project. If you apply the credit, the refund guarantee closes; the two are mutually exclusive. Two weeks, because ad account data moves fast — your campaigns look different a month out, and so does the diagnosis.

Not a fit if: your ads have been running less than 2–3 months. There is not enough history to read.

Pricing Structure

How the price gets fixed

Most creative pricing is a range that moves. Ours does not.


The audit defines the scope: how many products, what source material exists, how many characters and settings the work requires. Once that is agreed, the price is fixed in writing before production starts.


If the scope changes, the price is recalculated against a published rate — not renegotiated. You will always know what a change costs before you approve it.


No "starting from". No mid-project adjustments.


The audit fee is credited against production if you move forward within two weeks of receiving the report: fully toward a launch package, half toward a monthly system. Two weeks, because the findings date quickly — a month on, your account has moved and half the report no longer applies.

Terms

Clear terms, no annual lock-in.

Monthly systems run on a two-month minimum, then month to month.


No annual contract. No platform fee. No prepayment for the year.


Cancel with 30 days notice — by email, not by process.


The two-month minimum exists for one reason: the second cycle is where the work compounds. Anything shorter and you are judging a first draft.


Marketing services run on a 3-month minimum, then month-to-month with 30 days' written notice to exit. Ad budgets are paid directly to the platforms — not through us. All ad and analytics accounts are created in your company's name, and we'll tell you exactly who on our side is running them.

How Pricing Works

We price the creative system, not the number of files.

Avenquell pricing reflects creative direction, visual planning, production complexity, review depth, product accuracy requirements, and how the assets will be used across campaign channels.


A simple visual audit is scoped differently from a launch package or a monthly creative system. Every engagement is shaped around the business use case first.
